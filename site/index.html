Research with care

Use tools on software and devices you own or have permission to inspect. Laws, licenses, service terms, and anti-cheat rules vary; check them before you begin.

01 · Choose a questionStudy behavior, compatibility, a file format, accessibility, preservation, or a security issue within an authorized scope.
02 · Work from lawful inputsUse your own builds, open-source samples, vendor SDKs, or explicitly authorized test targets. Keep game assets, firmware, keys, and personal data out of this hub.
03 · Record evidenceDocument tool versions, provenance, observations, uncertainty, and reproducible steps. Separate facts from hypotheses.
04 · Share responsiblyRespect each project’s license and disclosure process. No DRM or access-control bypass, anti-cheat evasion, or unauthorized paid content.

How the hub stays current

GitLab is the planned canonical home. A scheduled check watches the listed upstream repositories and proposes metadata-only merge requests when their commits move. Every change gets human review; nothing is silently merged or copied into this catalog.
